# CTO 1 — Investment decision

Issue / product / package version / scope hash / human-message reference:

Every claim: FACT | INFERENCE | ASSUMPTION | UNKNOWN; dated source URL and method, limitations and contrary evidence.

Five questions:
1. Who experiences which concrete problem, how often and how severely?
2. What do they use/pay for today; what contradicts our hypothesis?
3. Why would this proposition make them switch?
4. How can we reach them within the budget?
5. What observed usage/payment commitment supports investment?

Desk research informs hypotheses. Without observed target-user behavior, willingness to use/pay remains UNKNOWN. No guarantee of “certain validation”.

Evidence experiment: hypothesis | audience | measurable behavior | predeclared pass/stop threshold | budget | owner | authorization needed. User outreach, paid traffic and publishing require explicit authorization.

Options: proceed to limited design / gather evidence / park / stop. Recommendation and uncertainty. Gate 1 does not authorize development.
